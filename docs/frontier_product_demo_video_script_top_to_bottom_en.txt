TrustLayer - Product Demo Script (Top-to-Bottom Flow, 90s)

Format:
- Voiceover: what you say
- Screen: what you record

00:00-00:10 | Header (Start at the Top)
Voiceover:
"This is TrustLayer. At the top, the Methodology button explains our scoring logic, and the API button shows live backend health for transparency."
Screen:
- Start with the page at the very top.
- Hover the logo/name briefly.
- Click Methodology, keep it open 2 seconds, close it.
- Click API, briefly show /health JSON in new tab, then return.

00:10-00:22 | Top Tokens Table
Voiceover:
"Here is our Top Tokens table for Solana. It gives a fast market overview with token identity, market stats, and risk context in one place."
Screen:
- Keep focus on the table area.
- Move cursor across 2-3 rows.
- Highlight one row with hover.

00:22-00:32 | Filters
Voiceover:
"You can narrow results using filters to focus on the exact subset of tokens you want to review."
Screen:
- Use available table filters (for example search/tag filter).
- Show filtered results update.
- Reset back to full list.

00:32-00:44 | Select a Coin
Voiceover:
"Now I select one token from the table to open a detailed risk analysis on the right."
Screen:
- Click one token row in the table.
- Wait for right panel to refresh.

00:44-01:00 | Right Panel Explanation
Voiceover:
"The right panel shows risk score, confidence, explainable signal breakdown, and warnings, so users can understand not just the result, but why it was generated."
Screen:
- Keep right panel in focus.
- Point at score card, confidence, and warning sections.
- Briefly scroll to show breakdown and diagnostics.

01:00-01:08 | Market Pulse
Voiceover:
"Below the table, Market Pulse summarizes the broader condition of tracked tokens."
Screen:
- Scroll down to Market Pulse.
- Pause on key metrics.

01:08-01:16 | Leaderboard
Voiceover:
"The leaderboard highlights key movers and risk shifts at a glance."
Screen:
- Move to leaderboard block.
- Hover one or two entries.

01:16-01:24 | Liquidity vs Holder Concentration Map
Voiceover:
"This map plots liquidity against holder concentration to quickly reveal potential structural outliers."
Screen:
- Move to the Liquidity vs Holder Concentration map.
- Hover several points so tooltips are visible.

01:24-01:30 | Close
Voiceover:
"TrustLayer gives a full top-to-bottom workflow: discover, filter, analyze, and interpret risk in one interface."
Screen:
- Hold a clean final frame with insights area visible.

Recording Notes:
- Record in 1080p, 30fps.
- Keep mouse movement slow and intentional.
- Pause one second after each click.
- Do one full clean take, then re-record only short sections if needed.
